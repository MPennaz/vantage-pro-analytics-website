## What VPA Is

**Vantage Pro Analytics** is a cloud-native manufacturing intelligence platform that combines ERP, MES, QC, and AI-powered analytics into one modern system — purpose-built for small and mid-sized manufacturers.

**One-line pitch:** The manufacturing platform that gives small and mid-sized manufacturers the tools Fortune 500 companies take for granted — without the cost, complexity, or 18-month implementation timelines.

**Tagline:** Smart Manufacturing. Simple Tools.

## The Problem We Solve

Small and mid-sized manufacturers ($5M–$200M revenue, 25–500 employees, 1–5 sites) are stuck between two bad options:

1. **Spreadsheets and tribal knowledge** — cheap but fragile. Inventory counts are wrong, lot traceability is a nightmare, audit prep takes weeks, and nobody has real-time visibility into anything. This works until it doesn't, and it stops working right when the company starts growing.

2. **Enterprise ERP systems** (SAP, Epicor, Infor M3) — powerful but crushing. $200K–$2M+ to implement, 12–24 months to deploy, requires consultants to maintain, and the people on the floor hate using it because the UX was designed in 2005.

**There has never been a middle path.** VPA is that middle path.

### Pain Points We Hit (Use These in Copy)

These are real things manufacturers say. Use this language — it resonates because it's their lived experience:

- "Where's that lot? Check the spreadsheet. No, the other one."
- "We shipped the wrong material. Nobody caught it until the customer called."
- "The auditor is here. Pull the records. All of them. By Friday."
- "We got quoted $500K just for the ERP implementation."
- "Our QC process is paper-based. If we lose the binder, we lose the data."
- "I have no idea what's actually in inventory right now."
- "We spend 2 weeks preparing for every audit."
- "Production, QC, and shipping are in three different systems that don't talk to each other."
- "We know we have problems, but we can't afford a $2M ERP project."

## What VPA Includes

### Core Platform (ERP)
- **Item Master** — SKU management, lot control settings, item types (raw, WIP, FG, packaging, MRO), specs, substitutes, images, documents
- **Inventory & Lot Traceability** — Real-time on-hand by item, lot, location, and site. Full lot traceability from receipt to shipment. Centralized inventory ledger — every move (receipt, issue, transfer, adjustment, scrap) goes through one system
- **Purchasing** — Purchase orders, receiving, supplier management. Every receipt tied to its PO line with lot tracking
- **Sales Orders** — Customer orders, pricing, order status tracking, approval workflows
- **Shipping** — Pick, pack, ship with lot-level detail. Carrier and tracking management
- **Bill of Materials (BOM)** — Revisioned BOMs with tree visualization, where-used analysis, formula and assembly support. Status-controlled (Draft → Active → Obsolete)
- **Customer Management** — Customer records, contacts, ship-to/bill-to sites, agreements, customer-specific products and pricing
- **Supplier Management** — Supplier records, contacts, preferred supplier items, lead times

### Industry Modules (Add-Ons)
- **Process / Chemical MES** — Batch processing, formula management, raw material issuance, finished goods output, yield tracking
- **Discrete MES** — Work orders, routings, labor tracking (future)
- **Advanced QC** — Inspection workflows, spec management with parameters (target/min/max), lot disposition, COA generation support
- **Advanced Analytics & AI** — Deep analytics, automated insights, predictive alerts

### AI & Document Intelligence (Built-In)
- Natural-language queries — ask questions about your data in plain English
- Document intelligence — upload spec sheets, COAs, MSDSs and get instant AI-powered analysis
- Role-aware insights — a plant manager sees different things than a QC tech or a sales rep
- This is NOT a bolt-on. AI is woven into the platform from day one.

## Brand Voice & Messaging Guidelines

### Tone
- **Confident but not arrogant.** We know what we're talking about because we've been there. We don't need to trash competitors — we let the product speak.
- **Direct and clear.** Manufacturing people don't have time for marketing fluff. Say what it does. Say why it matters. Stop.
- **Empathetic to the pain.** We've seen the spreadsheet chaos, the audit scrambles, the ERP sticker shock. We speak their language because we lived their reality.
- **Warm but professional.** We're a small team building something real. That authenticity is an asset, not a weakness.

### Words We Use
- "Real-time visibility"
- "Lot traceability"
- "Modern" (vs. legacy)
- "Cloud-native"
- "Purpose-built for manufacturers"
- "AI-powered" (not "AI-driven" or "AI-enabled")
- "Deploy in weeks"
- "The tools Fortune 500 companies take for granted"
- "Smart Manufacturing. Simple Tools."

### Words We Don't Use
- "Disrupt" or "disrupting" — overused, means nothing
- "Synergy" — corporate speak, not our voice
- "Best-in-class" — everyone says this
- "Revolutionary" — let the product prove it
- "Cutting-edge" — vague and overused
- "Leverage" (as a verb) — say "use"
- "Solution" as a standalone noun — say what it actually is

### Headlines That Work for VPA
- "Your Manufacturing Operation Deserves Better Than Spreadsheets."
- "And you shouldn't need a $500K ERP project to get it."
- "See your inventory. Trace your lots. Ship with confidence."
- "One platform. Every department. Real-time."
- "Built by people who've run real plants — for people who run real plants."
- "The ERP your team will actually use."
- "Audit-ready in seconds, not weeks."

## Landing Page Structure (Recommended)

The landing page should follow this flow — it mirrors the buyer's thought process:

1. **Hero** — Headline + subheadline + CTA (Schedule a Demo). Lead with the pain or the promise, not the product name.
2. **Problem Section** — 3–4 pain points in their language. Make them feel seen.
3. **Solution Overview** — What VPA is, in one paragraph. Then a visual or grid of the key modules.
4. **Differentiators** — Why VPA vs. alternatives. Keep it scannable (icons + short descriptions or a comparison table).
5. **Social Proof / Credibility** — Team background, years of experience, the "built by manufacturers" story. Testimonials when available.
6. **How It Works** — Simple 3–4 step process (Schedule Demo → Discovery → Onboarding → Go Live). Reduces perceived complexity.
7. **CTA Section** — Repeat the demo CTA. Consider a secondary CTA (download the one-pager, watch a 2-minute overview).
8. **Footer** — Contact info, vantageproanalytics.com, copyright.

## What the Landing Page Should NOT Do

- Don't lead with features. Lead with the problem or the outcome.
- Don't show pricing. Use "Contact us" or "Schedule a Demo" as the CTA.
- Don't use stock photos of people in hard hats pointing at tablets. If you use imagery, use screenshots of the actual product or abstract/branded visuals.
- Don't write paragraphs when a sentence will do. Manufacturing people scan — they don't read marketing essays.
- Don't compare to specific competitors by name. Position against categories ("legacy ERP", "spreadsheets") not brands.
- Don't use jargon that isn't manufacturing jargon. Technical manufacturing terms are fine (lot, BOM, WIP, COA). Marketing jargon is not (synergize, leverage, paradigm).
- Don't hide the team. Your credibility is your biggest asset right now. The founder's manufacturing background should be prominent, not buried in a footer.
